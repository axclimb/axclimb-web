Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This privacy policy governs the processing of the personal data of users of the Kumbra mobile application (hereinafter, "the App"), in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 of 5 December on the Protection of Personal Data and Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDGDD). In the event of any discrepancy, the Spanish version shall prevail.

1. Data controller

The controller of the data is Álvaro Montero Carmena, domiciled in 28935 Madrid, Spain (hereinafter, "the Controller"). For any enquiry regarding data protection, users may write to axclimb@gmail.com.

2. Personal data processed and purposes

Data categorySourcePurpose
Account data: email address, password (stored in encrypted form), first name, first surname and username. Provided by the user when registering or when updating their personal details. Creating and managing the account, authentication, password recovery and displaying the profile.
Sports activity data: climbing gym or crag, date, duration, routes and boulders climbed (grade, result, attempts and style), session rating, notes and, where applicable, metres climbed. Entered by the user in the App. Keeping the user's history, calculating level, experience points, statistics and rankings, and publishing the activity to other users.
Photos and videos that the user adds to their sessions. Selected or captured by the user in the App. Displaying them with the session on the user's profile and, if the user so chooses, in other users' feeds. They are reduced in size on the device before being uploaded.
Social interaction data: users followed and followers, recognitions ("kudos"), muted or blocked users, reports submitted and their reason, and favourite places. Generated through use of the App. Providing the social features, enforcing blocks and mutes, and reviewing reports to keep the community safe.
Places added by the user: name of the gym or crag, town, province, discipline and, optionally, its location. Provided by the user when a place is not yet listed in the App. Allowing sessions to be logged at that place and, after review, making it available to other users.
Notification data: the device's notification identifier, language, App version and notification preferences. The user's device, with prior authorisation. Sending notifications about social activity (kudos and new followers).

Waiting list

Anyone who joins the App's waiting list (www.axclimb.com/kumbra) provides their email address and the username they wish to reserve; if they arrive through another person's link, the username of the person who invited them is also recorded. This data is processed on the basis of consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for the sole purpose of reserving that username, confirming the sign-up, announcing the App's launch and recognising founder status. It is kept until the person creates their account or, at most, six months after the launch, and can be deleted at any time on request. It is stored in the App's database (Supabase, European Union) to reserve the username and, when the person creates their account with the same email, to recognise them as a founder.

Data that stays on the device

The following data is processed exclusively on the user's device and is not transmitted to the Controller's servers:

  • Location, used only when the user looks for nearby places, unless the user chooses to attach it to a place they add.
  • Apple Health data (heart rate, active energy and workouts), read only if the user enables the connection and solely to show the user the data of their sessions. The App does not write data to Apple Health or use it for advertising. As an exception, the metres climbed that the user chooses to save with a session are stored together with it.
  • Reminders and the Live Activity on the lock screen.

Usage metrics

The App sends anonymous usage events to its database (for example "session saved" or "session shared"), with a random installation identifier and the App version, not linked to the account, the email or the sessions. They are used to understand which parts of the App are used in order to improve it. They are kept for a maximum of 24 months.

Device permissions

The App requests access to the camera, microphone, photos, location (only while in use), Apple Health (read only) and notifications only when needed for the corresponding feature. Users may refuse or revoke these permissions at any time in the iOS settings.

The Controller does not display advertising, does not sell or transfer data for commercial purposes, does not track users across third-party apps or websites and does not use third-party analytics tools.

3. Information visible to other users

Kumbra is a social network. The user's first name, the initial of their first surname, username, avatar, level, followers and following, and sessions (including their notes) are visible to other users of the App on the profile, in the feed, on place pages and in the rankings. The email address is never shown to other users. A session's photos and videos are shown to other users unless the user chooses to show them only on their own profile. If the user shares a session on other platforms, it is done at their own decision and through the third-party app they choose. Blocked users lose access to the profile and activity of the user who blocked them.

4. Legal basis for processing

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): account, sports activity and social interaction data, necessary to provide the requested service.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): notifications, location and Apple Health. Consent may be withdrawn at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): handling reports, preventing abuse, keeping the service secure and anonymous usage metrics to improve it.
  • Compliance with legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR), where applicable.

5. Recipients

Data is not disclosed to third parties, except to the following processors, with whom the corresponding agreement is in place:

  • Supabase, Inc.: database hosting, account management and sending account emails. Data is hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: storage and delivery of session photos and videos, which can only be accessed through signed, time-limited links.
  • Resend, Inc.: sending account emails and internal report alerts.
  • Apple Inc.: delivery of notifications to the device.
  • Google LLC (Gmail) and Vercel Inc.: receiving waiting-list sign-ups and sending the confirmation email, and hosting the sign-up page, respectively.

Any international transfers outside the European Economic Area are carried out with the safeguards provided for in the GDPR, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Data may also be disclosed to the competent authorities where required by law.

6. Retention period

Data is kept for as long as the account remains active. When the user deletes their account from the App (Settings → Delete account), the account, profile, sessions, social activity, photos, videos and registered devices are deleted immediately. Videos are deleted automatically twelve months after being published, and only their preview image is kept. The provider's backups are overwritten within a maximum of 30 days. Data stored only on the device is deleted when the App is uninstalled.

7. User rights

Users may at any time exercise their rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and data portability. Account details can be corrected and sessions deleted directly in the App; erasure can be requested from Settings → Delete account.

To exercise these rights, users may send their request to axclimb@gmail.com from the email address associated with their account. Requests will be answered within one month at the latest. Users also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or with the supervisory authority of their country of residence.

8. Minors

The App is intended for people aged 16 or over. If the Controller becomes aware that an account belongs to a child under 16, it will delete it.

9. Security

The Controller applies appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect the data: communications between the App and the servers are encrypted with HTTPS, passwords are stored in encrypted form and the database access rules prevent users from modifying other users' data.

10. Changes

The Controller may amend this policy to reflect changes in legislation or in the service. Significant changes will be notified to users through the App or by email before they take effect, and the date shown at the top will be updated.
