Delete your Kumbra account

Last updated: 9 October 2026

You can delete your Kumbra account and all of its data at any time, from the app or, if you no longer have it installed, by email.

From the app

  1. Open Kumbra and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and its data are deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email axclimb@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete account” and your username. We will check that the address matches the account, delete it within 30 days and confirm it by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details.
  • Your profile: name, username and photo.
  • Your sessions, with their problems and routes, notes, photos and videos.
  • Your social activity: followers and following, kudos, comments and messages.
  • The devices registered to receive notifications.

What is kept

  • The server provider's backups, which are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Anonymous usage data that is not linked to your account.
  • What the app stores only on your phone, until you uninstall it.
If you have Kumbra Pro, deleting your account does not cancel the subscription. Cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or on your iPhone (Settings → your name → Subscriptions) so it doesn't renew.
